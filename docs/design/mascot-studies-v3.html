Rig

Every pose is about ten numbers and every transition is a spring. That maps one-to-one onto Rive state machine inputs or a SwiftUI Canvas driven by a timeline.

look.x · look.y
Eyes slide around the cylinder, so they squash near the edge. The body leans after them and the tip lags behind.
squash
Volume-keeping stretch on a soft spring: breathing, hops and boops all jiggle.
attention
Pointer distance. Get close and the eyes widen and it spins up a little.
spin
Flute phase. 0.35 idle, 14 drilling. The flutes are real helices, so they wrap around the edges; past about 4 they pick up a vertical motion blur.
eye.top · eye.bottom
One path: dot, blink, ^ happy, flat-topped focus, one-eyed squint.
mouth
0 hides it. Talking drives it from a level, which could be the voice room's audio meter.
dots · chips

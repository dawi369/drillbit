Drillbit mascot studies

Five takes on a little buddy for the app. Move your pointer and they'll look at you. Tap one to make its day.

Every moving part is its own group, animated with CSS transforms only, so each part maps to one layer in Rive, Lottie or SwiftUI shapes. Idle blinks and breathes, happy hops, thinking looks up and types, drilling squints and spins. Reduced motion freezes everything on the current mood.

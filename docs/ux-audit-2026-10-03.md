# UX audit — 3 October 2026

Scope: the whole iOS app after the first-run rebuild, with a deep pass on voice. Evidence comes from a full simulator UI tour (20 UI tests with screenshots, iPhone 17 Pro class, dark and light), a read of the SwiftUI sources and the Worker's voice route. Nothing here is physical-device verified. Findings marked *(code)* were read in source; *(sim)* were seen in simulator screenshots.

Priorities: **P0** breaks trust or the core loop, **P1** costs conversion or clarity, **P2** polish.

## Fixed in this pass

- **Ticket settles late after closing the preview** *(sim, code)*. Home opened the preview with a zoom transition. On dismissal its snapshot of the ticket sat for about a second with the transition's own shadow, then snapped back to the ticket's printed shadow. The preview is now a plain sheet. [HomeView.swift](../apps/ios/Drillbit/HomeView.swift)
- **Bit is quieter.** Home header 104 → 72 pt, tucked beside the title. The question preview shows Bit only while the question is being written, then fades it out. Before, a 72 pt Bit sat in the footer for the whole preview and overlapped Start interview while the button arrived. Completion 88 → 64 pt, Welcome 220 → 180 pt, sign-in 190 → 140 pt. The rule is now in [architecture.md](architecture.md): small unless Bit is the subject, and present for a reason.
- **Welcome glow had hard top and bottom edges** *(sim)*. The radial gradient was clipped to Bit's frame. It now draws past the frame and fades out.
- **The forced walkthrough fixture could never reach sign-in.** This was test-only, and the walkthrough test passes again.
- **Two ambiguous UI tests.** The inline “Need a nudge?” versus the menu item, and a racy loading assertion.
- **Docs said voice keeps “daily minutes”.** It is 6 starts per UTC day.

## Across the app

### First run and Welcome

- **P1. Voice is missing from the promise.** Welcome sells “Grills you like the real one”, but the most realistic thing the app does, talking it through out loud, appears nowhere before the interview screen *(code: no voice copy in Welcome, Setup, Home or the preview)*. See Voice below.
- **P2. Welcome and sign-in show Bit back to back.** At the new sizes this is acceptable. If sign-in later gains Apple-only copy, consider dropping Bit there and keeping the logo.

### Home

- **P1. Rep actions hide in a long-press menu** *(code)*: Regenerate, Choose focus or level, Choose another question and Skip. VoiceOver gets Skip as a custom action, but sighted users have no visible hint. Add a small “···” or “Not this one?” affordance on the stub that opens the same menu.
- **P2. Two phrasings for one idea**: “picked from your last answer” for the queued question versus “picked from today’s answer” after a rep *(code)*. Pick one, preferably “picked from today’s answer”.
- **P2. The free-rep waiting ticket is a dead end** *(sim)*: “Free reps, one a week, on the house”, with no action. This is correct until Superwall lands. The stub is where the `practice_rep` placement belongs, for example “Want more? See plans”.

### Question preview and preparation

- **P2. “Session style” appears as both the section header and the row label** *(sim, code: PreparationView)*. Drop the row label or rename the section to “How it runs”.
- **P2. Placeholders suggest gimmicks** *(code)*: “(e.g. talk like a pirate)” in preparation and “Pirate voice welcome…” in Settings. They are playful, but they teach the wrong use of the field on a prep screen. Use an example that improves practice, such as “e.g. push me on the data model”.

### Interview (text)

- **P1. The navigation title is the generic “System design”** *(sim)*. Use the question title, which is the thing you're working on. It already appears above as the original question, so a short form works.
- **P1. The voice entry is a 20 pt glyph in the composer footer** *(sim, code)*, with no label and only inside an already-started text interview. This is the main discovery problem for voice.

### Feedback, recall and library

- **P1. Feedback metadata reads like a system log** *(code: LibraryView, PracticeEvidence)*: “No explicit help recorded”, “Independence not established”, “Assistance was available”. Rewrite in the Welcome voice: “Solo run, no hints”, “You had hints on”, “Not sure yet if you can do this alone”.
- **P2. The Library eyebrow “Questions / evidence”** *(code)* is an internal model name. Use “Your questions” and keep “Practice evidence” as the row title.
- **P2. Settings is reachable from both Home and Library's toolbar** *(code: RootView)*, crowding Library's toolbar next to its own filter and actions. Consider keeping Settings on Home only.

### Cross-cutting

- **Terminology is consistent in what users see**: Guided, Practice and Mock interview. The legacy Solo, Coach and Guided `AssistanceMode` survives only in the unreachable `PracticeView`/`HelpView` code path *(code)*. This is dead code; delete it in a separate change.
- **Bit rule**: at most one animated Bit per screen, small unless it is the subject. Never in the answer workspace or the voice room.
- **Not yet audited on device**: haptics, audio routes, Dynamic Type at the largest sizes in voice, and real network latency.

## Voice — make it the premium main room

### Where it stands (verified)

- **Entry**: only from inside a started text interview, through the unlabelled waveform glyph *(code: InterviewView footer)*. The preview's only call to action is “Start interview”, which means writing.
- **Before Start** *(sim)*: an orb labelled “Ready” and a Start button. Nothing says what will happen, how long it can run, how many starts are left, or that headphones help.
- **In the room** *(sim, code)*: the primary amber control is **Mute**. Ending voice is the navigation bar's **Back**, which hides a stop action behind a navigation word. The [voice room plan](voice-room-plan.md) specified an explicit “End voice” in the fixed controls and Cancel while connecting; neither exists. There is no elapsed time and no indication of who is speaking beyond the orb.
- **Limits** *(code: voice.ts)*: 6 `voice_start` per account per UTC day, and each session is reserved for 15 minutes. A start is consumed **before** the provider connects, so a failed connection still burns one. The client only learns “unavailable” plus a reason. When the limit is hit, the user sees an alert saying it “resets at midnight UTC”, which is the wrong clock for everyone outside UTC. Nothing shows the remaining starts beforehand.
- **Cost shape**: starts don't bound minutes. One account can hold up to 6 × 15 minutes of live audio a day, and audio cost scales with duration.
- **Turn-taking**: there is no barge-in story in the UI. The user mutes or waits.
- **Feedback**: the debrief is the same as for text. Nothing about spoken delivery, such as pace, filler words, structure, talk ratio or long silences.
- **Delegation**: substantive feedback goes to a text model with a deadline and its own `voice_reasoning` limit. The spoken model paraphrases it. Users can't see this, which is fine, but slow delegations look like dead air.
- **Acceptance**: real-device audio, interruptions and route changes are recorded as open in [voice-foundations.md](voice-foundations.md).

### Direction

Voice is the closest thing to the real interview, and it is the thing worth paying for. Treat it as the headline of the paid tier and give free users one real taste.

1. **Make voice a way to start, not a mode you discover.** The preview's footer gets two equal choices, **Talk it through** (voice) and **Write it** (text), with voice first for subscribers. Home's ticket stub can say “Talk it through” when voice is available. Welcome gets one line, such as “Or say it out loud, like the real one.” The footer glyph stays as the mid-interview switch, but labelled “Talk”.
2. **A pre-flight card, shown once per session.** It covers what happens (“The interviewer asks, you talk. Interrupt any time.”), the time budget for this session, the remaining allowance, and a headphones hint. It also asks for the microphone at this point, with an explanation, rather than cold.
3. **A room built around the conversation, not the mute button.**
   - The fixed controls become **End** (primary, explicit, ends voice) and **Mute** (secondary). Show Cancel while connecting, as the plan already specifies.
   - Add a quiet elapsed or remaining clock, which mocks need anyway.
   - Show speaking state from real audio only: “Interviewer” or “You”, under the orb.
   - Add an optional live caption line, the last interviewer sentence, so you never have to open History to catch a word you missed.
   - Show a “Thinking…” hold while delegation runs, so dead air reads as intentional.
4. **Turn-taking that feels human.** Use server voice-activity detection with barge-in, so talking over the interviewer stops its audio. Leave a short grace before the interviewer jumps in, and offer a “give me a sec” gesture: hold the mic button to think without being prompted.
5. **Voice mock rounds.** Add phases (clarify, high level, deep dive, wrap-up) with the round clock, and have the interviewer move you along. This is the most interview-like thing in the product and the clearest paid feature.
6. **Spoken-delivery debrief.** Alongside What worked and What to fix, add pace, filler rate, how long before you committed to an approach, and talk ratio. Each point quotes the transcript. Keep it to one coaching point, not a dashboard.
7. **Allowance in minutes, honest and local.**
   - Meter minutes, not starts.
   - Refund a start when the provider handshake fails.
   - Show the remaining minutes before Start and warn about 2 minutes before the cutoff, with a spoken heads-up.
   - Show the reset in the user's local time.
   - Superwall: a `voice_start` placement that gates Start for free users after their taste.
8. **Reliability you can see.**
   - Instrument the time to the first interviewer audio, the connect success rate, the delegation latency and the reasons sessions end, as aggregates without content.
   - Offer one tap to reconnect after a drop, keeping the transcript.
   - Run device acceptance before any marketing claim.
9. **Accessibility.**
   - VoiceOver announces state changes only (connected, muted, ended, time warnings), never the transcript.
   - Add a haptic on connect, mute and time warnings.
   - Provide a large-type layout for the room's controls.

### Free and paid split (proposal)

- **Free**: one voice session with the free rep, about 5 minutes, enough to feel the room, then a soft paywall at End: “That's what the real one feels like.”
- **Paid**: voice on every rep, voice mock rounds, the spoken-delivery debrief, and a monthly minute allowance (sized after measuring real session lengths).
- **Server**: `VOICE_UNLIMITED_ACCOUNTS` keeps working as the allowlist until Superwall entitlements arrive. This mirrors the practice gate.

### Suggested order

1. Quick fixes in the room: an End button in the fixed controls, Cancel while connecting, the reset in local time, refunding a failed start, and labelling the footer glyph “Talk”.
2. Voice as an entry choice in the preview, plus the pre-flight card. Measure how many people pick it.
3. A minutes allowance and remaining-time display (API, OpenAPI, fixtures and DTOs), plus the `voice_start` placement hook.
4. Captions, speaking state, the Thinking hold and barge-in.
5. Voice mock rounds, then the spoken-delivery debrief.
6. Device acceptance and instrumentation throughout, and before the paid launch.

### Open questions

- How large is the free taste: one session per free rep, or one lifetime?
- Should subscribers see voice as the default on the preview, or as an equal choice?
- What is the minute budget per month for paid users? It needs real session-length data first.
